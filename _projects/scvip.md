bullets:
  - "Handles partially observed, inconsistently defined phenotypes and batch effects across cohorts"
  - "Predicts developmental age (r = 0.95), Huntington's disease burden (CCC = 0.90), Alzheimer's staging, and rheumatoid arthritis state"
  - "Cell-type-aware attention pinpoints the populations behind each phenotype, such as stress-responsive microglia and inflammatory T cells"
